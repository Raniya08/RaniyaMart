# RaniyaMart — User Manual & Role Walkthrough Guide

## 1. Getting Started
Visit the live portal at: `https://raniyamart.onrender.com`

## 2. Buyer Guide
1. **Browse Products**: Navigate to the public catalog (`/products`) to explore items across Electronics, Beauty, Fashion, and Furniture.
2. **Search & Filter**: Enter keywords in the search bar or select categories from the dropdown.
3. **Add to Cart**: Click **View Details** and specify the desired quantity to add products to your cart.
4. **Checkout**: Proceed to `/checkout`, select your payment method (UPI, Card, or COD), and confirm order placement.
5. **Track Orders**: View your completed orders under `/orders`.

## 3. Seller Guide
1. **Log in**: Access `/seller/dashboard` using your seller credentials.
2. **Manage Listings**:
   - Create new listings with custom titles, prices in ₹, stock quantities, and descriptions.
   - Use the 1-click sample image buttons (🧴 Sunscreen, 📱 Phone, 💻 Laptop, ⌚ Watch, 🪑 Furniture) to instantly assign high-quality visuals.
3. **Fulfill Orders**: Monitor incoming buyer orders and update status from `CONFIRMED` to `SHIPPED` and `DELIVERED`.

## 4. Admin Guide
1. **Access Dashboard**: Log in with `admin@raniyamart.com` / `Password123!` at `/admin/dashboard`.
2. **Platform Oversight**: Review registered buyers and sellers, monitor inventory metrics, and audit system activity.
